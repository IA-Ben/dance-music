# fortyyearsof.dance

**Forty years of dance music, 1987–2027 — an interactive book by Ben Dawson.**

Read it at **https://fortyyearsof.dance**

A history of electronic dance music built as a linked book: a page for each of the forty years; techno, house, rave, jungle & drum and bass, and trance as strands of chapters, meeting at the crossings they share; a technology route through the machines and a strand on drugs and the law; every person, club, record, label, machine, station, event and landmark set a page of its own, linked both ways; working reconstructions of the hardware, a synthesiser in every chapter and a two-deck mixer at the end. Everything runs in the browser — no server, no build step.

Open `index.html` locally, or read it at https://fortyyearsof.dance.
