# Dance Music

**An interactive history of electronic music — by Ben Dawson.**

A history of electronic dance music built as a linked book: techno, house, rave, jungle & drum and bass, and trance as five strands of chapters, crossing at the border towns they share; every person, club, record, label, machine, station, event and landmark set a page of its own, linked both ways; a synthesiser in every chapter and a two-deck mixer at the end. Everything runs in the browser — no server, no build step.

Open `index.html`, or read it at the published site.
